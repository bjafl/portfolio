I am Bjarte Flø Lode, a social worker and job consultant in AFT (Arbeidsforberedende trening, work preparation training) at Sandnes Pro-Service. I also build software, and the two go together: what I know about the people and the work makes the tools better.

## Background

I started in engineering cybernetics, switched to psychology and ended up with a bachelor in social work. From the cybernetics studies I have all the calculus required for engineering, some physics and introductory courses in electronics and cybernetics.

IT and development have always been a strong interest. A couple of years ago I completed 60 ECTS credits of the bachelor in IT engineering at UiT, alongside a full-time job. It covered basic programming and computation, and second-year courses in machine learning and Linux.

## From work to development

It began with Excel, VBA and small scripts that made the working day easier for me and my colleagues. From there it moved on to Python, web apps and browser extensions. I have also done a lot of data processing, including with pandas, especially when new case-management systems were introduced and data had to be converted and consolidated. Because I know the organisation and its data, I can tailor the conversions and solutions to get the best result.

## What drives me

I want to work with people and help them toward a better everyday life and a better life overall. I enjoy finding creative solutions, and using domain knowledge to build technology that solves real problems and is tailored to the need.
